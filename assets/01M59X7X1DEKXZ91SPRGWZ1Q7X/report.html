Executive Summary

Insights and trends

The sample holds 29,059 URLs analysed between 4 Sep 2026 and 18 Oct 2026, of which 13,818 are gambling sites.

Slots and Casino are the two largest product types. Nearly all product assignments rest on reasoning inference rather than lexicon match (96.7%), so product figures are indicative only.

The gambling URLs reduce to 2,584 brand clusters, and 33 clusters cover half of them. The largest cluster spans several product types.

Many clusters rotate across more than one top level domain. Abbreviations and Affiliate & Agent are the most common signal tags.

URL volume arrives in separate bursts with low activity between them, so the period does not support a statement of direction.

No page in the sample shows a regulatory blocking indicator.

What a lexicon match means

A site is assigned to a subcategory by lexicon match when a word from the gambling keyword lexicon appears in what was recorded about the site, and that word belongs to the product category assigned. A description containing บาคาร่าออนไลน์ is assigned to Baccarat by lexicon match. The lexicon is the SICPADetect Thai gambling keyword lexicon at the version on the title page: 286 keywords in 14 categories, mostly in Thai script. Seven categories decide the subcategory and seven are reported as signal tags. A lexicon match rests on a word that can be pointed to, so it can be checked and repeated. An inferred assignment rests on the model reading of its own description and carries lower confidence.

Inferred share 96.7%; General Gambling share 15.8%. The lexicon has poor coverage of this dataset, so subcategory figures are indicative only.

Brands and clustering

What brand clusters are

What a cluster is

A brand cluster is a group of domains judged to belong to the same gambling brand or operator. Domains join a cluster when the crawler found one as a variant of or redirect from another, when their names reduce to the same stem once market tags, version numbers and suffixes are removed, so siam855thb5.com and siam-th.net both reduce to siam, or when their stems are near identical in spelling. A domain that matches no other is a singleton.

Why clusters matter

Operators rarely run a single site. They register many domains, rotate top level domains and move players between them, so that when one address is blocked another takes its place. Counting URLs overstates how many operators there are. Clustering shows how many distinct operations exist and which run several product types, and acting on a cluster reaches every domain in it. Clusters are an analytical grouping based on names and crawler links, not a legal finding about ownership, and the linkage table is indicative only.

Key insights

Scale of the analysis

29,059 distinct URLs were analysed over 45 days (2026-09-04 → 2026-10-18). 13,818 are gambling, 15,241 are not gambling and 0 are unreachable.

The sample is large enough to reason about patterns, not isolated cases.

What is being offered

The three leading product subcategories are Slots (3,977, 28.8%), Casino (3,368, 24.4%), General Gambling (2,184, 15.8%). 15.8% of gambling sites are confirmed as gambling without an evidenced product type, and 96.7% were assigned by inference rather than keyword. 209 lexicon keywords never matched. The lexicon has poor coverage of this dataset, so subcategory figures are indicative only. Categories with no matched keyword: Vague-Evasion Terms, Hashtags.

Slots is the leading product type, but at 28.8% the estate is spread across product types rather than concentrated.

Brand clustering

2,584 clusters span 13,818 gambling URLs. 1,222 hold more than one domain, and 33 clusters cover half the estate. The largest, 49m, holds 2,885 domains across 5 subcategories.

The estate is diffuse and per-URL blocking will have limited leverage.

How the estate is grown and marketed

867 clusters rotate across more than one top level domain. The two most common signal tags are Abbreviations (27.1%) and Affiliate & Agent (5.3%).

TLD rotation keeps a stable entry point while the sites behind it are replaced.

Enforcement position

0 pages already show regulatory blocking indicators. The set appears pre-enforcement and is a candidate for a first pass.

This set is a candidate for a first enforcement pass, prioritised by cluster rather than by URL.
