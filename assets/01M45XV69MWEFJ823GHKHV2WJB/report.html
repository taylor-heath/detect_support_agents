Executive summary

Insights and trends

SICPADetect analysed 5,816 distinct URLs during the pilot reporting period and classified 3,953 of them (68.0%) as gambling. The remaining URLs are not gambling and awaiting final classification.

Casino is the largest product subcategory at 50.2% of gambling sites, so one category accounts for most of the gambling set. General Gambling follows at 19.4%. Most subcategory assignments rest on model inference, so the subcategory figures are indicative.

A brand cluster is a group of domains that appear linked by naming pattern, redirect behaviour, crawler relationship or shared brand stem. SICPADetect identified 938 clusters, of which 442 hold more than one domain. The largest cluster holds 56 domains. Acting on one cluster can cover many related domains. The links are indicative and do not establish common ownership.

The data shows related domains registered under one brand stem, rotation across top level domains and redirects and crawler variants between domains. Prioritising by cluster covers more of this activity than acting on one URL at a time.

No URL in the analysed set carried blocking or enforcement indicators. MDES can include all identified gambling URLs in a first prioritised enforcement pass. Cluster-based prioritisation lets MDES act on groups of related domains instead of handling each URL separately.

Assignment method by subcategory

How each subcategory was assigned. Solid fill is a lexicon match and the lighter fill is model inference.

What a lexicon match means

SICPADetect assigns a subcategory by lexicon match when a recorded text field contains a keyword from the gambling lexicon, and that keyword belongs to the assigned product category. The recorded fields are the model-generated site description and classification rationale, extracted keywords, analyst remarks, and the URL and domain. A site whose recorded text contains สล็อต is assigned to Slots by lexicon match. When no product keyword appears, the model assigns the closest subcategory from what the description says the site offers. A lexicon match rests on a specific keyword found in the text, while model inference rests on the model’s reading of the site. The Thai gambling keyword lexicon holds 286 keywords in 14 categories, most in Thai script. The 7 product categories decide the subcategory. Other categories appear as signal tags. A lexicon match can be checked and repeated, so it carries higher confidence. A large model inference share means the sites use vocabulary the lexicon lacks.

Model inference accounts for 98.8% of gambling rows. General Gambling accounts for 19.4%. The lexicon covers part of the vocabulary used in this dataset, so subcategory figures based on model inference are indicative. Bar labels show lexicon match count, then model inference count.

Brands and clustering

What brand clusters are

What a cluster is

A brand cluster is a group of domains that appear linked by naming pattern, redirect behaviour, crawler relationship or shared brand stem. SICPADetect links domains in three ways. The crawler found one domain as a variant of, or a redirect from, another, and this crawler relationship is the strongest basis. The domain names reduce to the same brand stem once market tags, version numbers and suffixes are removed. Or the stems differ by only a few trailing characters. A domain linked to no other is a singleton. For example, 001-win.bet and 1-win.app reduce to the shared stem 1win.

Why clusters matter

Gambling operators often register many related domains, rotate top level domains and redirect users between them, so a new address can replace a blocked one. A URL count therefore gives more addresses than there are distinct operations behind them. Clusters show how many distinct operations appear in the identified gambling URLs, how concentrated those URLs are, and which clusters offer several product types. Acting on a cluster can cover all of its related domains in one step. Clusters are an indicative analytical grouping based on names and crawler links. They do not establish legal ownership, and the linkage table is indicative.

Key insights

Scale of the analysis

SICPADetect analysed 5,816 distinct URLs during the pilot reporting period (28 days, 2026-08-31 to 2026-09-27). Of these, 3,953 are gambling, 1,453 not gambling and 410 are awaiting final classification.

3,953 URLs (68.0%) of the URLs analysed are gambling.

What is being offered

The three largest product subcategories are Casino (1,985, 50.2%), General Gambling (767, 19.4%), Slots (639, 16.2%). 19.4% of gambling sites are confirmed as gambling without an evidenced product type. Model inference assigned 98.8% of gambling sites. 255 of 291 lexicon keywords never matched.

Casino is the largest product subcategory at 50.2%, so one category accounts for most of the gambling set.

Brand clustering

SICPADetect identified 938 indicative clusters across 3,953 gambling URLs. 442 clusters hold more than one domain. 106 clusters cover half of the identified gambling URLs. The largest, 1win, holds 56 domains across 5 subcategories.

The identified gambling URLs spread across many clusters, each holding a small share, so cluster prioritisation still groups related domains but each action covers fewer URLs.

How operators expand and promote their domains

Among gambling URLs, 3,048 crawler variants, 174 redirect-derived and 731 reached directly. The busiest redirect source is 1win-kwp1.buzz with 12 destinations. 218 clusters use more than one top level domain. The most common signal tags are Abbreviations (36.9%) and Affiliate & Agent (2.2%).

Operators use redirects and top level domain rotation to keep users reaching their sites when individual domains change.

Enforcement position

No URL in the analysed set carried blocking or enforcement indicators. MDES can include all identified gambling URLs in a first prioritised enforcement pass.

Cluster-based prioritisation lets MDES act on groups of related domains instead of handling each URL separately.
