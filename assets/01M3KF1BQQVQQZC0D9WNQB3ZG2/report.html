How market relevance was determined

A record is reported only if it carries gambling content and passes at least one relevance test. Test A is a direct market reference — chile anywhere in the domain (mid-string included), a .cl ccTLD or a cl host marker, or captured content citing Chile, the Chilean peso, a Chilean payment rail, a RUT, a +56 number, a Chilean regulator, operator or football reference. Test B is uncontested Spanish content in the reasoning field. Test C excludes on a competing locale.

Spanish is the working language of twenty countries, so Spanish vocabulary alone is never treated as a Chile signal. Spanish and Portuguese are discriminated on vocabulary that differs between them (apuestas/apostas, cuenta/conta, jugar/jogar, dinero/dinheiro, ruleta/roleta, tragamonedas/caça-níqueis). Depósito is spelled identically in both and is never used as a signal.

Limits and data quality

Two caveats that govern how this report may be used.
  1. Inheritance is an assumption, not a finding. VAT_PAID means the domain descends from a registered platform — not that the domain itself sits inside that registration. A typosquat trading on a registered brand while operating outside its declared estate looks identical in this data. This is the group most needing verification.
  2. VAT_NOT_PAID means unlisted, not confirmed unregistered. An operator may hold a registration that is absent from the supplied reference lists. Nothing in this report establishes that any operator has failed to register.

Observations on the reference lists

  • The VAT reference file was not supplied with this request. The request carried the report specification and the detection export only. The mapping in this report therefore uses the registered-platform and unregistered-brand lists as they are set out in the client's own report specification corpus (24 registered platforms, 10 listed brands). If the canonical reference file differs from those lists, the mapping must be re-run before the figures are relied on.
  • Corporate entries with no consumer-brand annotation. skillonnet.com, baytreeinteractive.com and 418services.com appear on the registered list as corporate entities with no consumer brand beside them. The brands they operate are not guessed here, so any of their consumer domains detected in this window default to VAT_NOT_PAID. This is a gap in the reference data, not a finding about those operators.
  • Mixed granularity on the registered list. Entries mix bare registrable domains (betway.com), subdomains (cl.novibet.com, cl.bet7k.com) and platform-numbered domains (betsson1001.com). A numbered entry does not propagate to its successors: a domain such as betsson1002.com matches nothing on the list and falls to VAT_NOT_PAID even though it belongs to the same estate.
  • Duplicate entry. latamwin.online appears twice in the source list; it is one platform and is counted once.
  • The blocked-site list was not supplied as a separate input. The replication reference in the specification (76 domains across 23 brands) was used to scope which parent brands are in play; it is not used for any legality claim.

The residual

14,139 of the 14,433 gambling records (98.0%) match neither reference list, so the list-scoped part of this analysis says nothing about them. Within the reported set that residual is 272 URLs (76.0%). Scoping to a supplied list leaves the large majority of monitored activity unassessed on the VAT axis.
