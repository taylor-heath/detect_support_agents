Executive summary

Insights and trends

The estate analysed holds 21,978 URLs, of which 9,858 are classed as gambling sites. Daily volume is uneven across the evaluation period, with a few days of heavy analysis, so no direction is stated.

Slots is the leading product type, at 32.3% of gambling sites. Product assignments rest mostly on reasoning inference rather than a lexicon match, so subcategory figures are indicative only.

Operators are diffuse: 78 brand clusters hold half of all gambling URLs.

The estate is grown by registering many domains per brand. 769 clusters rotate across more than one top level domain, and Abbreviations is the most common signal tag.

7 pages already show blocking indicators.

No ranked gambling site in this sample carried Thai audience evidence.

Assignment method by subcategory

How each subcategory was assigned. Solid fill is a lexicon keyword match and the lighter fill is reasoning inference.

What a lexicon match means

A site is assigned by lexicon match when a word from the gambling keyword lexicon appears in what was recorded about the site, and that word belongs to the assigned product category. A description containing สล็อต is assigned by lexicon match to the category that word belongs to. The lexicon is the SICPADetect Thai gambling keyword lexicon at the version on the title page, with 286 keywords in 14 categories. Seven decide the subcategory and seven are signal tags. A lexicon match rests on a word that can be pointed to, so it can be checked. An inferred assignment rests on the model reading of its own description, so it carries lower confidence. A large inferred share means the sites use vocabulary the lexicon does not yet cover.

Inferred share 96.0% of gambling rows. General Gambling share 14.6% of gambling rows.

Brands and clustering

What brand clusters are

What a cluster is

A brand cluster is a group of domains judged to belong to the same gambling brand or operator. Domains join a cluster in one of three ways. The crawler found one as a variant of, or a redirect from, another, which is the strongest basis. Their names reduce to the same stem once market tags, version numbers and suffixes are removed, so a3game.app and a3game.click both reduce to a3game. Or their stems are near identical in spelling. A domain that matches no other is a singleton.

Why clusters matter

Operators rarely run a single site. They register many domains, rotate top level domains and move players between them through redirects, so that when one address is blocked another takes its place. Counting URLs therefore overstates how many operators there are and hides who is behind the estate. Clustering shows how many distinct operations exist, how concentrated the estate is, and which operators run several product types. Acting on a cluster reaches every domain in it, where acting on a single URL leaves the rest of the brand running. Clusters are an analytical grouping based on names and crawler links, not a legal finding about ownership, and the linkage table is indicative only.

Key insights

Scale of the analysis

21,978 distinct URLs were analysed over 32 days, 4 Sep 2026 to 5 Oct 2026. 9,858 are gambling, 12,120 not gambling and 0 unreachable.

The sample is large enough to reason about patterns, not isolated cases.

What is being offered

The leading product types are Slots (3,182, 32.3%), Casino (2,609, 26.5%) and Football Betting (1,711, 17.4%). 14.6% of gambling sites are confirmed as gambling without an evidenced product type. 96.0% were assigned by reasoning inference. 209 lexicon keywords never matched.

The estate is spread across product types rather than concentrated, with Slots the largest. The lexicon covers little of this sample, so subcategory figures are indicative only.

Brand clustering

2,100 clusters sit across 9,858 gambling URLs. 1,087 hold more than one domain and 78 clusters cover half the estate. The largest, a3game, holds 507 domains across 4 subcategories.

The estate is diffuse, and per URL blocking will have limited leverage.

How the estate is grown and marketed

769 clusters rotate across more than one top level domain. The two most common signal tags are Abbreviations (34.5% of gambling sites) and Marketing & Acquisition (3.8%).

TLD rotation keeps a stable brand while the addresses behind it are replaced.

Enforcement position

7 pages already show regulatory blocking indicators.

A measurable share is already under enforcement.
