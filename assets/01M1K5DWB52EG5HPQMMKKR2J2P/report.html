Group E — Key insights

Scale of the analysis

The standard set holds 648 distinct URLs over an evaluation period of 1 day (2026-08-27 → 2026-08-27), of which 118 are gambling sites, 336 are not gambling and 168 were unreachable.

The sample is large enough to reason about patterns, not isolated cases.

What is being offered

The three largest product subcategories are Football Betting (38 sites, 32.2%), Slots (35 sites, 29.7%), Casino (19 sites, 16.1%). 9.3% of gambling sites are confirmed as gambling without an evidenced product type (General Gambling). 61.9% of subcategories were inferred from reasoning text rather than matched on a lexicon keyword. 260 of the 286 distinct lexicon keywords never matched. Because more than 30% of assignments are inferred, the lexicon has poor coverage of this dataset and the subcategory figures are indicative only.

Football Betting leads, but at 32.2% of gambling rows the estate is spread across product types rather than concentrated.

Brand clustering

66 clusters span the 118 gambling URLs; 23 hold more than one domain and 15 clusters cover half the estate. The largest, moenas, holds 7 domains across 1 product subcategory.

The estate is diffuse: 15 clusters are needed to cover half of it, so per-URL blocking will have limited leverage.

How the estate is grown and marketed

0 gambling URLs are crawler-identified variants, 48 sit behind redirect chains and 70 were reached directly. The busiest redirect source, localcoffeemontclair.com, points at 6 destinations. 14 clusters appear under more than one top-level domain. The two most common signal tags are Abbreviations (40.7%) and Marketing & Acquisition (15.3%).

Redirects and TLD rotation keep a stable entry point while the sites behind it are replaced.

Enforcement position

No page in this sample carries a regulatory-blocking indicator, so the set appears pre-enforcement. 5 pages matched a blocking phrase only because the crawl met an anti-bot challenge; those are access failures, not enforcement, and are excluded.

This set is a candidate for a first enforcement pass, prioritised by cluster rather than by URL.

Methodology

Subcategories inferred from reasoning text rather than matched on a lexicon keyword: 61.9% (inferredShare). Gambling sites confirmed without an evidenced product type: 9.3% (generalShare).

Clustering thresholds: seed linkage takes precedence; otherwise identical name stems group together; a near-miss merge requires Jaro–Winkler similarity ≥ 0.90 with one stem a prefix of the other. Signal-keyword corroboration is reported at Jaccard ≥ 0.60 and never merges clusters.
