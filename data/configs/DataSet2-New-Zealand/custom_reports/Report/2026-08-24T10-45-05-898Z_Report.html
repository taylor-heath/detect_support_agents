Section D — Key insights

1. Scale of the analysis

9,502 distinct URLs were analyzed over the 43-day evaluation period (2026-07-13 → 2026-08-24), of which 7,950 were illegal gambling sites and 4 were licensed.

Take: the sample is large enough to reason about patterns, not isolated cases.

2. Status mix

Illegal gambling represents 83.7% of all URLs analyzed and 99.9% of gambling sites (licensed + illegal). 1,548 URLs were not gambling and 0 were unreachable.

Take: illegal operators are the majority of the gambling sites detected.

3. Variants and brand abuse

2731 brands were cloned via variant domains; the most-abused brand t80.bet spawned 16 variants.

Take: A handful of brands drive most proliferation; prioritize enforcement against them.

4. Redirects and traffic shaping

67 illegal URLs use redirect chains; the busiest source c37.club points to 14 destinations.

Take: Redirects keep a stable entry point while rotating sites behind it.

5. Enforcement and regulatory relevance

No pages in this sample show regulatory-blocking indicators (court orders, regulator notices, block pages) in their reasoning text — the set appears pre-enforcement.

Take: This set is a candidate for a first enforcement pass.
